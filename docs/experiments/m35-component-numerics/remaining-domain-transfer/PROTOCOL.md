## Validity and sufficient positive bridge

Each arm must complete its full generation budgets, finite complete heads and
rows, paid coverage with zero violations, and proven retirement. Speculative
arms must actually draft and accept. Observe every proposal and verify row,
including rejected suffixes, with no bounded-trace overflow. Spill and restore
must preserve every initialized stage exactly without model recomputation;
continuation must reuse the recorded history.

Within each build, default/full-head transport must preserve proposals,
verification inputs/verdicts, kept logits, tokens, histories, counters and state.
Only the declared output representation may differ: transport kind, raw bytes,
head width/head hashes and proposal-row indices newly exposed by full heads.
Head-map identity and mapped proposal tokens remain exact. Across full-head
candidate/reference arms require exact full-head and verify-row hashes,
proposals/verdicts, kept outputs, histories/counters and initialized state.
Qwen's plain arms require exact full heads, all kept rows, histories/tokens and
three state images. Equality is a sufficient numerical/state bridge. An
unexpected difference stops that exact screen for attribution; it does not
by itself establish a quality regression or permit a post-result tolerance,
golden refresh or additional inference under this registration.

Full installed plan inventories and runtime `completed_plan` events prove dose.
Snapshots alone are insufficient. Match actual uses to exact inventories,
including private ID0 lifetimes and reclaimed plans. Independently review
logical roots, component/stage ownership, encodings, K/N, actual columns,
views/strides, preparation, GLU order and state writes. Collapse only proved
changed ordinary subgraphs; preserve every unaffected relationship and
implementation identity after a narrowly authenticated crosswalk. The compiler
identity includes planner objects, so the three explained planner object
changes alter the module digest despite identical kernel objects. Reconstruct
each observed implementation identity from its actual declaration and the two
exact module digests, preserving all other fields. Unknown identities or
additional differences invalidate the contrast; no wildcard normalization.
Do not zip unequal recipe lists or drop all Q8 preparation. No new comparator
framework is required.

Qwen must execute its ordinary five-column prefill. DeepSeek must execute
ordinary target prefill/verify and stage1 drafting. Gemma arms must execute
their real assistant heads and target verification. Missing dose, a surviving
affected reference VecQ, MMQ/unsupported upstream fallback, missing inventory,
changed policy or unexplained routed/grouped/wide difference invalidates the
comparison. Preserve the failure; do not adjust prompts to rescue coverage.
